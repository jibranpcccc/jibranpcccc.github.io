DMCA Copyright & Trademark Takedown Protocol

We respect the intellectual property rights of others. While our directories index publicly available invite links and summaries rather than hosting digital media files, we comply promptly with legitimate Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and trademark takedown notices.

Required Elements for a Formal Notice

To submit a legally valid takedown request, please email our designated agent at jibranpccc@gmail.com with the following details:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work or trademark claimed to have been infringed, including registration numbers if applicable.
  2. The exact URL on our domain or Blogger blog where the infringing link or description appears.
  3. Your full legal name, company or organization, physical mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
  4. A statement that you have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notification is accurate, and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner of the exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.
  6. A physical or authorized electronic signature of the copyright owner or legal representative.
