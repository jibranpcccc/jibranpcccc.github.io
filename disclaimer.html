2. Cryptocurrency & Web3 Airdrop Risk Disclaimer

Cryptocurrency assets, decentralized finance (DeFi) protocols, non-fungible tokens (NFTs), and Web3 token airdrops are subject to extreme market volatility, technical smart contract vulnerabilities, regulatory shifts, and high fraud incidence. Free token drops and airdrops frequently attract phishing campaigns, malicious wallet-drainer scripts, and fraudulent Telegram bot impersonations.

Conduct your own independent research (DYOR) and consult a licensed financial advisor before allocating capital to any digital asset.
