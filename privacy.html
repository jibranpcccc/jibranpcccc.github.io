Privacy Policy & Data Governance

🔒 Architecture: Zero-Account Storage ⚖️ Standard: GDPR & CCPA Compliant 📅 Effective: September 2026

The Authority Directory Network, its affiliated domain portals, and the Blogger Authority syndicate are committed to absolute user privacy. We believe that discovering quality communities and professional opportunities should never require surrendering your personal data.

1. Zero-Account Policy & Data Minimization

Our platforms are strictly architectural zero-account environments. We do not require, offer, or maintain user registrations, passwords, profiles, or personal contact databases. You can browse all directory listings, copy community invite links, filter categories, and inspect platform metrics without creating an account or providing an email address.

2. Information Collected & Analytics Governance

We do not collect names, telephone numbers, financial details, or physical addresses. The only electronic information processed during your session consists of:

  • Aggregate Traffic Metrics: We utilize Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: G-CK7NVYS1Y9) to evaluate anonymous traffic volume, geographic distribution at the country level, device types, and referral engines. IP addresses are automatically anonymized by Google before storage.
  • AI Search Referral Attribution: Our client-side script detects incoming referrer headers from modern generative engines (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot) to understand how AI search engines cite our directory nodes. No personal identifiers are linked to these events.
  • Web Server Access Logs: Standard HTTP server logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp, requested URI) are temporarily recorded by GitHub Pages and Google Blogger infrastructure for network security, DDoS mitigation, and firewall operation.

3. Cookie Disclosures & Local Storage

We do not deploy marketing, advertising, cross-site profiling, or behavioral tracking cookies. Standard session tokens used by Google Analytics 4 (_ga, _ga_*) are strictly functional for unique visitor counting and expire in accordance with standard browser defaults. You may disable cookies completely in your browser settings without impacting your ability to navigate our directories.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance Disclosures

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Know & Access: Because we operate a zero-account architecture and do not correlate IP logs with personal identities, we maintain no individual personal records.
  • Right to Erasure (Be Forgotten): If you submit a community via our submission form or send an inquiry via email, you may request the immediate deletion of your correspondence by emailing our data compliance desk.
  • Sale of Personal Data: We never sell, rent, lease, or monetize user data under any circumstances.

5. Third-Party Platforms & External Links

Our service provides direct referral links to third-party communication platforms including Telegram, Discord, WhatsApp, YouTube, and independent software sites. Once you navigate away from our domain via an invite link, your interaction is governed entirely by the privacy policy and terms of the respective external platform. We encourage you to review their data handling practices before joining external groups.

6. Data Protection Inquiries

For questions or formal requests regarding our privacy architecture, contact our Data Governance Officer at desk@authoritydirectory.org.
